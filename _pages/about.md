I am an undergraduate in Software Engineering at
[Fudan University](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/), graduating in June 2027.
I am applying to Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

I work on the structure that sits between a language model and the task it is given: reasoning
steps that could live in hidden states instead of emitted tokens, skills that relate to one another
as a typed graph rather than a flat library, and development that decomposes into features rather
than a single prompt. Across all three the recurring question is the same — what structure is the
model already relying on implicitly, and what changes once you make it explicit and let the model
act on it directly?

Lately that has meant probing latent reasoning until the probes yield something you can actually
intervene on at decode time, and handing agents a typed skill graph they can query and edit while
they work rather than a retrieval pipeline fixed in advance.
